01 / OVERVIEW

Critical experience becomes a learning loop.

DriveFoundry couples what the system learns from a scene with how the model decides what to compute next. The same scene-candidate record connects evidence, behavior and consequence across data production, training and inference.

DATA ENGINE

Structured causal supervision

Failure mining finds rare interactions. VLM-assisted annotation proposes World-Policy-Value records, experts correct the critical relations, and quality gates publish reusable supervision.

Follow the data loop →

MODEL COGNITION

Value-guided computation

Xiaomi-Drive-o1 shares one vision-language backbone across Perception, Causal Reasoning, Action, World and Value. Value decides when additional reasoning earns its cost.

02 / METHOD

One backbone. Five interfaces. Shared latent state.

Block-wise autoregressive latent refill passes realized upstream outputs into downstream task blocks. The model can be fast when the scene is routine and deliberate when a candidate needs more evidence.

P

Perception

Objects, geometry, detection, segmentation and depth.

C

Causal Reasoning

Critical factors, roles and decision relations grounded in evidence.

A

Action

Semantic decisions, meta-actions and continuous trajectories.

W

World

Candidate-conditioned future visual evidence at the endpoint.

V

Value

Safety, comfort, efficiency and a recommendation for more compute.

03 / ADAPTIVE INFERENCE

Reason only as far as the decision requires.

Value is both a critic and a scheduler. It accepts a good candidate quickly, requests targeted reasoning for uncertain scenes, or escalates to full candidate-conditioned world verification.

SYSTEM 1

Action → Value

Generate and score a candidate from the shared driving knowledge already encoded in the backbone.

SYSTEM 2

Perception → CoC → Action → Value

Refill scene evidence and causal relations before regenerating the candidate.

FULL THINKING

... → World → Value

Predict candidate-conditioned future visual evidence, then rank admissible trajectories with safety fallback.

04 / DATA ENGINE

World - Policy - Value, in one scene-candidate record.

The record keeps the factual scene, the candidate behavior and the consequence assessment together. Multiple candidates can share one scene, making counterfactual comparison explicit instead of post-hoc.

W

World

Candidate-relevant facts, object states and interaction relations with traceable evidence.

P

Policy

Semantic intent, lateral and longitudinal decisions, and their continuous motion realization.

V

Value

Hard constraints, soft preferences, observed outcomes and counterfactual judgments with provenance.

05 / TRAINING

A common interface from language to behavior.

Training moves from broad driving knowledge to structured task supervision and then to feedback that balances behavior quality with computation. The interfaces remain shared across all three stages.

STAGE 01

Driving knowledge

Language and discrete visual sequences establish perception, understanding and value judgment.

STAGE 02

Structured supervision

World, CoC, planning, future-world and Value labels connect semantic concepts to physical motion.

STAGE 03

Behavioral feedback

Driving returns and computational cost shape candidate quality and when to spend more reasoning.
